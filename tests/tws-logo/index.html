B1 — CHANNEL

THE SEQUENCE CUTS THROUGH

The closest heir to option B. Custom-drawn slab letterforms are split by a horizontal channel and the sequence runs inside it — through the letters, not behind them. Nodes surface in the gaps; the current exits past the S.

B2 — WEAVE

OVER · UNDER · OVER

Solid letterforms, and the current weaves them — passing in front of the T, behind the W, and in front of the S, where it rides exactly along the S's middle stroke before exiting. The weave gives the flat mark physical depth.

B3 — STEP-UP

THE WINNING TRAJECTORY, INSIDE THE MARK

Each letter is split at a different height — low through the T, mid through the W, high through the S — and the sequence staircases upward through all three, exiting above where it entered. Winning, built into the monogram.
